# 🦊 MetaMask

**MetaMask** is a browser and mobile wallet that lets you hold crypto, connect to dApps, and sign transactions and messages. It works with any **EVM-compatible** chain (Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Base, etc.).

> 📖 **Full walkthrough:** For a step-by-step intro to wallets, keys, gas, and DeFi basics, see **[MetaMask & Wallet Fundamentals](/weekly-presentations/subpage-1)** in our weekly presentations.

---

## 🎯 What MetaMask Is (and Isn’t)

### ❌ MetaMask is NOT
- A place where your coins “live”
- A bank or custodian
- The blockchain itself

### ✅ MetaMask IS
- **Key manager** — Stores your private keys (encrypted with your password)
- **RPC client** — Talks to blockchains via the provider (e.g. `window.ethereum`)
- **Signer** — Signs transactions and messages so the network accepts them

The **blockchain** holds the state (balances, contracts). MetaMask only holds keys and sends signed transactions. If MetaMask disappeared, your accounts would still exist; you’d recover them with your seed phrase in another wallet.

---

## 🔑 Keys and Seed Phrase

| Concept | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Seed phrase** | 12 or 24 words that derive all your keys. Back it up offline; never share it. |
| **Private key** | Proves ownership of an address. Never share; required to sign. |
| **Address** | Public identifier (e.g. `0x1234...`). Safe to share; used to receive funds. |

Whoever has the **seed phrase** controls every account derived from it. Use a **strong password** for MetaMask; it only encrypts the wallet locally and does not protect you if the seed is leaked.

## 🌐 Adding Networks and Using dApps

- **Add a network** — Settings → Networks → Add network (or “Add a network manually”). You need the chain ID, RPC URL, and optionally block explorer.
- **Switch chain** — Click the network dropdown in MetaMask; dApps can also request a switch via `wallet_switchEthereumChain`.
- **Connect a dApp** — The dApp calls `eth_requestAccounts`; MetaMask shows a connect prompt. You can revoke site access in Settings → Connected sites.

For building dApps that connect to MetaMask, see [EVM DApp](/web3/evm/dapp) (e.g. [Wagmi & RainbowKit](/web3/evm/dapp/wagmi-and-rainbowkit)).

## ⛽ Gas and Fees

- **Gas** — Units of computation; you pay in the chain’s native token (ETH, MATIC, etc.).
- **Gas price** — Price per unit; higher usually means faster inclusion.
- **Gas limit** — Cap on units; unused gas is refunded.

MetaMask lets you adjust gas (or use “market” estimates). For more on gas and DeFi flows, see [MetaMask & Wallet Fundamentals](/weekly-presentations/subpage-1).

## 🔐 Security Basics

- **Never share** your seed phrase or private key (except in controlled dev/test setups).
- **Verify URLs** — Phishing sites mimic MetaMask or dApp UIs; always check the address bar.
- **Revoke approvals** — Use [Revoke.cash](https://revoke.cash/) or similar to revoke token approvals when you no longer use a dApp.
- **Use a hardware wallet** for large holdings; MetaMask supports Ledger and other devices.
