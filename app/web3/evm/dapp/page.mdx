# 📱 DApp (EVM)

Building a **dApp** on the EVM means connecting a frontend (React, Next.js, etc.) to wallets and smart contracts. This section covers two popular stacks: **Scaffold-ETH 2** (all-in-one) and **Wagmi + RainbowKit** (modular).

---

## 🎯 What You Need

- **Wallet connection** — MetaMask, WalletConnect, Coinbase Wallet, etc.
- **Contract reads/writes** — Via [ethers](https://docs.ethers.org/v6/) or [viem](https://viem.sh/)
- **Chain switching** — Support Ethereum, Sepolia, Polygon, etc.
- **Account state** — Address, balance, network
