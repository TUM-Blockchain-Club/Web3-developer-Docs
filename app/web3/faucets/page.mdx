# 💰 Faucets

Use **faucets** to get **free testnet and devnet tokens** for development and demos. No real money involved — just enough to pay gas/fees and interact with dApps and contracts.

---

## 🎯 How to Use

1. Pick the **network** you’re building on (e.g. Sepolia, Solana devnet).
2. Open the faucet link and follow its steps (wallet address, captcha, sometimes social login).
3. Request tokens; they usually arrive within minutes.
4. Use them in your wallet to deploy contracts, send txs, or test your dApp.

## ✅ Tips

- **Save your wallet address** — Many faucets need it; use a dedicated dev wallet.
- **Rate limits** — Faucets often limit how much you can request per day; request only what you need.
- **Wrong network** — Make sure your wallet is set to the correct testnet/devnet before requesting.
- **Build first** — See [EVM](/web3/evm) or [Solana](/web3/solana) for development setup, then use faucets to fund that environment.
